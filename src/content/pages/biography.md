---
title: Biography
description: Swiss artist and art educator, based in Zurich, working primarily with painting.
---

Miriam Strauss is a Swiss artist and art educator (MA Art Education, Zurich
University of the Arts). Born in 1988, she works primarily with painting while
incorporating a range of other media. Her practice is shaped by an ongoing
engagement with environmental issues and the human relationship with nature.

She works both independently and in collaboration with others, exploring
different approaches through exchange, improvisation and the interplay of diverse
visual languages. Several extended periods abroad have significantly influenced
her practice and professional network.

Miriam regularly presents her work and processes in exhibitions and has been
nominated for various awards. She is based in Zurich, where she has taught Visual
Arts at Kantonsschule Zürcher Oberland since 2018.
